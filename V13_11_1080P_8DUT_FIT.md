# v13.11 - 1920x1080 8-DUT Fit

MAC Writer dashboard is compacted for a 1920x1080 production monitor.

- All 8 DUT cards are visible simultaneously.
- DUT 1-4 remain on the left and DUT 5-8 on the right.
- Label scan fields remain visible for every DUT.
- Status, MAC, Serial, GPON, PCB SN, process steps and View Log remain visible.
- The router scrollbar automatically hides when everything fits.
- Scrolling remains available as a fallback on smaller displays.
- Existing same-IP/dedicated-NIC routing and production logic are unchanged.
